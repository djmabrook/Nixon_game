# Nixon_game

An experimental, dreamlike third-person game in Godot 4.7. Small protagonist, big weird world. You are the little man.
A sand spiral at night leads to rooms, and every room breaks one rule.

- Open `project.godot` in Godot 4.7 or newer and press F5.
- Move with WASD or the left stick, look with the mouse or right stick, run with Shift. Hold Q or right mouse to look toward the landmark. Interact with E, Space or A.
- Read [CONVENTIONS.md](CONVENTIONS.md) before adding a room or anything else.
